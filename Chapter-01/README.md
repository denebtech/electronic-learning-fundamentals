# Chapter #1 - From electrons to electronics

## Concepts

**Electricity** is simply the movement of electrons through a conductor.

**Electron** is a particle with negative electric charge.

**Proton** is a particle with positive electric charge. Protons and electrons atract between its.

![atom](images/atom.png)

**Resistance** is the messurement of the ability of electrons to move through a material.

- *Conductors:* material that let electric current and electrons flow through them easily due a low electrical resistance (as marbels in an ice-skating rink). Examples of materials: copper, aluminum.

- *Insulators:* Materials that block or severely restrict the flow of electric current due to high electrical resistance (as marbels in sand). Examples of materials: plastic, glass.

![Conductor and Insulator](images/conductor_insulator.png)

The attactive force between positive and negative charges is an electromotive force called **voltage**.

The flow of electrons is refered to as an **electric current**.

Electricity concepts can be represented with the water pressure and pipe diameter analogy.

- Incresing water pressure (+ voltage) causes more water (electrons) to flow through the pipe (conductor).

- Using a larger diameter pipe (+ resistance) let more water (electrons) to flow through the pipe for a given amount of pressure (voltage).
